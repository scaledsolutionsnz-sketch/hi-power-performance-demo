Privacy Policy

Placeholder policy for this demo site. Final wording is confirmed with Hi-Power Performance before launch.

1. What we collect

When you contact Hi-Power Performance we may collect your name, phone number, email address and details about your vehicle so we can quote and carry out work. This website itself does not use contact forms.

2. How we use your information

Contact and vehicle details are used to respond to enquiries, book work, order parts and keep records of work performed. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

3. Third-party services

This site embeds a Google Map and loads fonts from Google. Those services may set their own cookies and collect usage data under Google's privacy policy. Links to Facebook, Instagram and YouTube open on those platforms under their own policies.

4. Storage and security

Customer records are kept only as long as needed for warranty, accounting and legal purposes, and are protected with reasonable technical and physical safeguards.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Call 021 119 0360 and we will sort it out.
