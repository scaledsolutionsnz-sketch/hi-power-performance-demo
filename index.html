Questions

The things people ask us

The questions that come up on the phone most weeks. If yours is not here, call the workshop and ask Ethan directly.

What types of vehicles do you specialise in?

We are a high-performance workshop built around Japanese platforms, with our reputation made on the Nissan GT-R: R32, R33, R34 and R35. That is not all we do. We tune, fabricate for and build street cars, track cars and full race cars across Auckland, whatever the badge on the front.

Do I need to do anything to my car before a dyno tune?

A good tune needs a healthy engine underneath it. Before we put a car on the dyno we check it over for boost leaks, worn plugs, fluid leaks and healthy compression. If you already know something is not right, get it fixed first. It is the difference between a safe tune and an expensive one.

Which aftermarket ECUs do you tune?

We tune Haltech and Link ECUs day in, day out, along with plug-in and standalone systems and factory ECU remapping. If you are not sure what is in your car or what you should be running, call us and we will talk it through before you buy anything.

Is a custom tune safe for a street car I drive every day?

Yes, and that is the whole point of doing it properly. Every tune we do is calibrated inside the safe thermal and mechanical limits of what is actually in your engine. A car should leave here making more power and still start, idle and drive like something you can use on a Monday morning.

Do you do fabrication and wiring for race cars?

We do complete motorsport prep in house: custom turbo kits, exhaust manifolds, intercooler and oil plumbing, and motorsport wiring looms built to survive a track weekend. If you have seen the fabrication work on our Instagram, that is all done here.

Can you help with LVV certification or WoF on a modified car?

Yes. New Zealand's rules around modified cars catch a lot of people out. We can prep your car, check where it sits against the requirements and carry out the work needed to get it through LVV certification and a Warrant of Fitness.

Do you do normal servicing, or only performance work?

Both. Plenty of cars come in for oil and fluid changes, brakes, clutches and diagnostics. If you trust us with the engine build, it makes sense to have the same people doing the servicing that keeps it alive.

Where are you, and can I drop my car off early?

We are at 14f Flexman Place, Silverdale, Auckland 0932, on the North Shore. Our hours are listed further down this page. Early drop-offs and late pick-ups are usually no problem, just sort it with us when you book so someone is expecting you.

How do I get a quote for an engine build or a full project?

Every build is different, so we would rather talk than throw a number at you. Email Ethan at ethan@hi-power.co.nz with your car, what you want out of it and any parts you already have, or call 021 119 0360, and we will map out the job and get you a workshop slot.

How long do you need my car for a tune?

Set aside a full day for a standard diagnostic and custom calibration. That gives us time to get the cold start right, do the steady-state mapping properly and finish with power runs. Fabrication work and engine builds get their own timeline, which we give you up front.
