What the workshop does

From a factory ECU retune to a full engine build, every job gets the same care as the shop's own 650kW R34.

Tuning

ECU & Dyno Tuning

Haltech and factory ECU tuning with real road and dyno time. You get the printout, not just a promise.

Engine

Engine Builds

RB, SR and JZ builds from refresh to fully forged. Measured, blueprinted and broken in the right way.

Electrical

Wiring Harnesses

Custom looms built for standalone ECUs and motorsport use. Tidy, labelled and built to last hard laps.

Fabrication

Custom Fabrication

Intercooler piping, exhausts, mounts and one-off brackets, made in-house to fit your car, not "close enough".

Motorsport

Race Car Preparation

Pre-event checks, setup and spanner support so the car is ready before you load the trailer.

Upkeep

Servicing, WoF & LVV

Servicing and maintenance that respects a modified car, plus WoF and LVV certification sorted for you.

Since 2016

Built around the cars we drive ourselves

Ethan Lowe opened Hi-Power Performance in Silverdale at the end of 2016 to work on the cars he grew up around: Skylines, GT-Rs and serious Japanese metal. The shop's own RB28-powered R34 GT-R makes 650kW and has been featured in NZ Performance Car.

That car is the standard. If a part, tune or weld would not go on the shop R34, it does not go on your car either.

  • GT-R and JDM specialists, not a general garage
  • Haltech and factory ECU tuning in-house
  • WoF and LVV certification handled for modified cars
